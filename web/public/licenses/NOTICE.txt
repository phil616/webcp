WebCP
An independent OpenResty website manager, licensed under GPL-3.0.

This program includes modified frontend components, SCSS styles and an SVG
menu background from 1Panel (https://github.com/1Panel-dev/1Panel),
commit 3adee9445897aa41c0160b4f805a9b1f43f712ac, licensed under GPL-3.0.
Copyright in those portions remains with the upstream 1Panel contributors.
Modifications for WebCP were made on 2026-11-11 and 2026-11-12.

The SSL frontend pages, models, ACME/DNS providers and CA helpers also reuse
1Panel source, adapted to this independent program. The Nginx lexer and generic AST
also reuse the same pinned 1Panel source. No panel authorization or account
management modules are included. This is not an official 1Panel product.

See web/panel/manifest.json and web/src/vendor/1panel/manifest.json for source paths, blob hashes and
per-file modifications, docs/gui-1panel.md for adaptation boundaries, and
LICENSE for the complete GPL text. The software is provided without warranty.
